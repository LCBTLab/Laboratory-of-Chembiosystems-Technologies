# Team Member Profile Management Guide

## 🎯 For Current Team Members

This guide explains how you can login and manage your profile on the ChemBioSystems Lab website.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. **Access the Login Page**

Visit: `https://your-site.netlify.app/login` (or your deployed URL)

### 2. **First Time? Create Your Account**

1. Click **"Sign up here"** at the bottom of the login form
2. Fill in:
   - **Full Name**: Your complete name (e.g., Dr. John Doe)
   - **Email**: Your official email address
   - **Password**: Choose a strong password (min. 6 characters)
3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You'll be automatically logged in

### 3. **Already Have an Account? Login**

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Click **"Sign In"**
3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

---

## 📝 Managing Your Profile

### Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your dashboard with:
- **Profile Summary**: Quick view of your information
- **Profile Status**: Shows if your profile is complete
- **Quick Actions**: Buttons to edit profile, view team page, etc.

### Edit Your Profile

Click **"Edit Profile"** button to update:

#### Basic Information
- ✅ **Full Name**: Your name as it should appear on the team page
- ✅ **Email**: Cannot be changed (contact admin if needed)
- ✅ **Designation**: Select from:
  - Principal Investigator (PI)
  - PhD Scholar
  - M.Tech Student
  - B.Tech Student
  - Research Staff
  - Intern
- ✅ **Phone Number**: Your contact number
- ✅ **Joining Date**: When you joined the lab

#### Research Information
- ✅ **Research Focus**: Your area of research (e.g., "Synthetic Biology, Metabolic Engineering")
- ✅ **Bio / About**: Write a brief bio about:
  - Your research interests
  - Academic background
  - Key achievements
  - Current projects

#### Academic & Social Links
- ✅ **LinkedIn**: Your LinkedIn profile URL
- ✅ **Google Scholar**: Your Google Scholar page
- ✅ **ResearchGate**: Your ResearchGate profile
- ✅ **ORCID**: Your ORCID identifier

### Save Changes

1. After filling in your information, click **"Save Changes"**
2. You'll see a success message
3. Your profile is now updated and visible on the team page!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Can't Login?
- **Check email/password**: Make sure Caps Lock is off
- **Forgot password**: Contact admin (feature coming soon)
- **Account doesn't exist**: Use "Sign up" to create account

### Profile Not Showing on Team Page?
- **Check completion**: You need Designation, Research Focus, and Bio
- **Check dashboard**: See "Profile Status" - must be "Complete"
- **Wait a moment**: Changes may take a few seconds to appear

### Can't Update Profile?
- **Session expired**: Logout and login again
- **Network error**: Check your internet connection
- **Validation error**: Make sure all required fields are filled
